# Serviceability and manufacturability

## Field service is part of the product

A design that only works for the engineer who built it is unfinished. Serviceability includes labeling, connector choice, replacement procedures, and diagnostic visibility.

## Manufacturability (DFM) and test (DFT)

- Prefer assemblies that can be tested in stages
- Design fixtures conceptually early—even when fixture drawings stay private
- Avoid “untestable” analog corners that only fail in seasonal humidity
- Document calibration and configuration steps as first-class artifacts internally

## Modularity with intent

Split modules when it reduces spare-part cost, certification boundary pain, or replacement time. Do not split modules merely to inflate SKU count.
